The Property

Waterfront living, designed around the Florida lifestyle.

This exceptional 3-bedroom plus dedicated office home pairs a spacious open-concept interior with generous outdoor living, privacy and direct sailboat access from an oversized corner lot.

Southwestern exposure brings sunset light across the screened lanai, heated saltwater pool and canal. Inside, zero-corner impact sliders, soaring ceilings and a generous KitchenAid kitchen connect the main living spaces naturally to the waterfront.
